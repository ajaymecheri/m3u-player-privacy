Privacy Policy

M3U Live Player for LG webOS TV · Effective date: 21 November 2026

In short: M3U Live Player does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.

1. Who we are

M3U Live Player (the "App", app ID com.ajay.m3uplayer) is a live-stream player for LG Smart TVs published by M3U Player ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the App handles information.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, location, contacts or payment details, and it does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking tools.

3. Information stored on your TV

The App may keep small amounts of data, such as playlists and settings, in the TV's local app storage so that they are available the next time you open the App. This data stays on your TV, is never sent to us, and is removed when you uninstall the App.

4. Third-party content and services

To show and play channels, the App connects directly from your TV to third-party servers:

  • the public channel list at iptv-org.github.io, when you press "Malayalam Live";
  • the servers that host each live stream you choose to play; and
  • the websites that host channel logo images.

Like any internet connection, these servers can see standard technical information such as your TV's IP address and the time of the request. We do not control these third parties, do not receive this information, and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their own policies.

5. LG platform

The App is distributed through the LG Content Store. Information LG collects about your TV or account is governed by LG's own privacy policy, not this one.

6. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Data sharing and sale

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not share it with or sell it to anyone.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
